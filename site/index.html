How to read this

The numbers
  • Buy: the average price you'd pay for the amount we suggest, walking up from the cheapest listing.
  • Sell, selling at home: the cheapest current listing in your faction's house, or its 7-day typical price if that's lower.
  • Sell, selling on Blackwater: half the other faction's price. Almost nobody buys on Blackwater to use the item; your buyers are traders from the other faction who resell it in their own house, and they need a profit too. If someone already sells the item on Blackwater for less than that, you can only match the cheapest Blackwater listing. Blackwater's own asking prices are otherwise ignored, because they are often far too high.
  • Back per 1g: what you get back for each gold you spend, after the cut (5% on your faction's house, 15% on Blackwater). ×2 means you double your gold. Solid is ×2 or more; Fair is ×1.15 to ×2.
  • Sold a day: how many units we saw sell where your buyers are, averaged over up to 7 days: your own faction's house when you sell at home, the other faction's house when you sell on Blackwater.
  • Market a day: sold a day × the price, so how much gold changes hands for that item each day.
  • Your share: we suggest moving at most a quarter of what sells each day, so you don't flood the market. Profit a day is your share × the profit on each item.
  • Busiest on Blackwater: items people buy on the neutral house that can be resold at a profit in the Alliance or Horde house, by gold changing hands each day. Resell in shows where, and what each 1g spent on Blackwater brings back there after the 5% cut. Items that cost more on Blackwater than either house pays are left out: those are mostly players moving goods between their own characters.
  • Sorting: tap a column heading to sort by it; tap it again to flip the order. The arrow shows which column and which way.
  • Chart search: type part of a name above the chart (for example “recipe”) to show and label only the items that contain it.
Where the data comes from

Every hour a free GitHub job reads the EU PvP Alliance, Horde and Blackwater auction houses from Blizzard's official Battle.net API. The API only shows what's listed, not what sold. We work out sales by comparing each snapshot with the one an hour earlier. A listing that disappears with 2+ hours left counts as sold, unless it looks like a cancel-and-relist or a cheaper listing of the same item is still there. Some cancellations will still slip through, so treat "sold a day" as an estimate. The first figures appear after one hourly comparison and settle over a day or two.

Not included: auction deposits (you lose them if an item doesn't sell, and Blackwater's are higher), and travel or mail time. Purchases on Blackwater arrive by mail.

Safety

Trade goods, not gold. Blizzard's rules forbid moving gold between factions, so buy and sell items on the auction houses, and click every purchase yourself.
