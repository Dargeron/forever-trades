    How to read this

    The numbers
    • Buy at: the average price you'd pay for the amount we suggest, walking up from the cheapest listing.
    • Sell at: the cautious selling price. It's the cheapest current listing on the other house, or its 7-day typical price if that's lower.
    • You get back: the sell price minus the auction cut, 5% on your faction's house and 15% on Blackwater.
    • Return (×): what you get back divided by what you pay. ×2 means you double your gold. Solid is ×2 or more; Fair is ×1.15 to ×2.
    • Sells a day: how many units we saw sell on the house you'd sell on, averaged over up to 7 days.
    • Your share: we suggest moving at most a quarter of what sells each day, so you don't flood the market.
    • Profit a day: your share × the profit on each item.
    • Days of stock: how many days the items already listed would take to sell. Lower is better.
    Where the data comes from

    Every hour a free GitHub job reads the EU PvP Alliance, Horde and Blackwater auction houses from Blizzard's official Battle.net API. The API only shows what's listed, not what sold. We work out sales by comparing each snapshot with the one an hour earlier. A listing that disappears with 2+ hours left counts as sold, unless it looks like a cancel-and-relist or a cheaper listing of the same item is still there. Some cancellations will still slip through, so treat "sells a day" as an estimate.

    Not included: auction deposits (you lose them if an item doesn't sell, and Blackwater's are higher), and travel or mail time. Purchases on Blackwater arrive by mail.

    Safety

    Trade goods, not gold. Blizzard's rules forbid moving gold between factions, so buy and sell items on the auction houses, and click every purchase yourself.
