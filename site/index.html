How this is worked out

Materials
  • For every material we take the cheapest way to get it on your side: buy it in your faction's auction house (walking up from the cheapest listing for the amount one craft needs), buy it from a vendor (Weak Flux, Coal, stocks, Engineer's Ink…), or make it: smelt ore into bars (Bronze from Copper and Tin, Steel from Iron and Coal) and make the parts yourself (bolts, tubes, gizmos, blasting powder, Gold Power Cores, Gyrochronatoms…). Open a recipe to see which way was chosen for each material, and what the other ways would cost.
  • Smelting assumes you have the Mining skill for it (Bronze 65, Iron 125, Gold 155, Steel 165, Mithril 175, Truesilver 230). Parts are made only when making them is cheaper than buying them.
  • When a material isn't listed right now, its recent price (3-day median or 7-day typical) is used and marked “recent”.
Selling
  • Sells for is the item's cautious price in your faction's house: the cheapest listing now, or its 3-day median, 7-day typical price or what buyers paid if any of those is lower. Profit per craft is everything one craft makes (some recipes make 2 to 200) times that price, minus the 5% auction cut, minus the materials. Auction deposits aren't counted.
  • Sold a day is an estimate. Profit a day assumes you sell a quarter of that.
  • A recipe with a big profit per craft but almost no sales may take a long time to sell. Recipes that lose money are listed too, at the bottom, and items that bind when picked up are left out (they can't be sold).
  • Recipes are WoW: Forever's own, read from Blizzard's game data: Forever changed several Classic recipes (the Mechanical Dragonling takes 4 Iron Struts, 4 Gyrochronatoms, 2 Citrines and Fused Wiring; rockets make 5) and added many (SAF-T and EZ-Thro devices, satchels of bombs, camping kits…).
  • Skill is the Engineering skill a recipe needs. Blizzard's data doesn't include it, so it comes from the Classic recipe list and, for some Forever recipes, from a Forever recipe guide. Recipes whose skill we don't know are hidden unless you tick “Forever recipes with unknown skill”. Schematics have to be found or bought first; Gnomish and Goblin recipes need that specialisation.
